Open Positions

We are actively seeking highly motivated students and researchers to join our team at PRLab, Nanjing University. Our lab focuses on cutting-edge research in visual understanding and generation, with a particular emphasis on video generation and diffusion models.

If you are interested in joining our group, feel free to contact me at chenyang.si@nju.edu.cn. For more information, please refer to this Zhihu post.
